Questions

Frequently asked

Does this apply to both online and live tournaments?

Yes - it's built for both. The Range Vault includes custom solutions for online ante structures and the BB ante typical in live events. Live tournaments have been my most-played format for the past six years.

I don't end up heads-up often. Why focus on it?

Heads-up doesn't only happen at the end - it happens constantly: SB vs BB, button vs blinds, IP vs BB. These are the highest-impact nodes in the game, and when you do reach the final two, that match is often worth 5-10% of the prize pool.

I don't use solvers. Will I be able to follow along?

Definitely. This was built for smart players, not just experts. Every concept is practical, visual, and immediately useful - you'll leave with a clear decision-making framework, no solver required.

Couldn't I just study solvers on my own?

You could, but it's a massive time investment. I've already put in hundreds of hours finding the patterns that work in real games, and distilled them so you can start using them right away.

Will this be outdated soon?

The core concepts are rooted in how people naturally play wide ranges - patterns that have survived multiple metas. I also explain why each strategy works, so you can adapt as the game evolves.
