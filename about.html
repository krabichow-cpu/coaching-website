About

Kevin Rabichow

Professional poker player, full-time coach, and a pretty good home cook.

I've played poker professionally since 2007, and I'm best known for my success in online heads-up cash games, where I earned over $1M in profit. In 2014 I started producing content with Run It Once Training, and from 2015-2017 I coached a small group of heads-up players to more than $500k in combined earnings.

In 2020 I launched a private coaching business and have spent hundreds of hours a year since helping players reach their potential. I now produce training content for GTO Wizard, commentate on tournaments for the Triton Super High Roller Series, and mentor poker players competing in some of the biggest games in the world.

My strength as a coach is to get to know your game, learn how you think, and identify your blind spots.

Away from poker, I love cooking for family and friends (I post some favorites on Instagram), and I'm an avid ultimate frisbee player and coach - a hobby that's made me a far better teacher.
